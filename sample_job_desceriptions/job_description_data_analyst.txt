JOB TITLE: Junior Data Analyst

The analyst will support reporting and business analysis by preparing datasets, identifying trends, and creating clear dashboards for stakeholders.

Required skills:
SQL, Excel, Python, pandas, data cleaning, data visualization, statistics, dashboards, analytical thinking, communication.

Responsibilities:
- Clean and validate data from multiple sources.
- Write SQL queries and prepare recurring reports.
- Build dashboards using Power BI or Tableau.
- Explain findings clearly to non-technical colleagues.

Preferred:
Experience with retail or customer datasets and a portfolio of analytical projects.
