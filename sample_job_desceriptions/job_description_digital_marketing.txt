JOB TITLE: Digital Marketing Associate

The Digital Marketing Associate will support online campaigns, content planning, search visibility, and performance reporting.

Required skills:
SEO, keyword research, Google Analytics, Google Ads, social media marketing, content writing, email marketing, campaign reporting, communication.

Responsibilities:
- Assist with SEO research and website content optimization.
- Prepare social media calendars and support campaign launches.
- Monitor engagement and campaign performance.
- Summarize metrics and recommend practical improvements.
- Coordinate content with the marketing team.

Preferred:
Experience using Canva and managing a small campaign or marketing portfolio.
